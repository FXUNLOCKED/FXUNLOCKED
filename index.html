Ever traded USD for Euros before a vacation? Congratulations, you’ve already traded Forex. In the markets, it’s just betting whether one currency gets stronger or weaker against another.

The 60-Second Primer

What is Forex, Really?

Short for Foreign Exchange (or FX), Forex is the global marketplace where world currencies are traded. With over $7 trillion exchanged daily, it is the largest and most liquid financial market on Earth—dwarfing all stock markets combined.

1. The Currency Swap

Forex trades in pairs. When you execute a trade, you simultaneously buy one nation's currency while selling another's.

2. Continuous Shifts

Values constantly move based on interest rates, inflation, job reports, and geopolitical news. Traders profit by anticipating these changes.

3. The 24/5 Rhythm

Global banking hubs pass trading volumes around the clock: Sydney → Tokyo → London → New York, non-stop Sunday evening through Friday.

Visual Anatomy

Cracking the Code: The Candlestick

Candlesticks reflect the real-time fight between buyers (bulls) and sellers (bears). Each candle has a rectangular Real Body and thin Wicks (Shadows).

Thick block = "Body" • Thin lines = "Wicks"
UPPER WICK / HIGH

The tip of the wick shows the highest price buyers achieved before meeting seller resistance.

REAL BODY

Shows the net move. In a green candle, the Open is at the bottom and the Close is at the top.

LOWER WICK / LOW

The bottom tail shows the deepest bargain price before buyers jumped back in.

Key Rule: Green candle = Buyers pushed price up (Close > Open). Red candle = Sellers dragged it down (Close < Open).
The Two Schools of Thought

Fundamental vs. Technical Analysis

Every trader relies on one of these styles (or combines both) to decide when to pull the trigger.

🏛️ The Macro View

Fundamental Analysis

Studies economic health, central bank interest rates, and geopolitical shifts. Asks: "Why is this currency moving?"

Primary Catalysts: Interest Rates, CPI (Inflation), GDP, Non-Farm Payrolls.
Ideal For: Long-term swing traders & macro position holders.
💡 Example: If the US Federal Reserve hikes interest rates, international investors often buy USD for higher yields, driving the Dollar up.
📐 The Tape Reader

Technical Analysis

Studies pure price action, chart patterns, volume, and statistics. Asks: "Where is price going next, and where do I place my stop?"

Primary Tools: Support/Resistance, Trendlines, Candlesticks, Moving Averages.
Ideal For: Day traders, scalpers, and intermediate breakout traders.
💡 Example: If EUR/USD bounces off a key support line three times in a row, a technical trader buys with a stop-loss just below that floor.

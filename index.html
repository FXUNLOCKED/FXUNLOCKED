Cracking the Code: The Candlestick

Candlesticks reflect the real-time fight between buyers (bulls) and sellers (bears). Each candle has a rectangular Real Body and thin Wicks (Shadows).

Thick block = "Body" • Thin lines = "Wicks"
UPPER WICK / HIGH

The tip of the wick shows the highest price buyers achieved before meeting seller resistance.

REAL BODY

Shows the net move. In a green candle, the Open is at the bottom ($65k) and the Close is at the top ($69k).

LOWER WICK / LOW

The bottom tail shows the deepest bargain price before buyers jumped back in.

Key Rule: Green candle = Buyers pushed price up (Close > Open). Red candle = Sellers dragged it down (Close < Open).

How Currency Quotes & Sessions Work

The mechanics of price pairs and the global 24-hour timezone schedule.

1. Base vs Quote

In EUR/USD = 1.0850, EUR is the Base (fixed at 1 unit), and USD is the Quote ($1.0850 needed to buy 1 Euro).

2. The Currency Tiers

Majors always feature the USD. Minors (Crosses) pair majors without USD. Exotics involve emerging markets.

3. Market Sessions

Sydney & Tokyo handle the Asian cycle. London & New York handle the Western volume. The London/NY overlap (8AM-12PM EST) is prime time.

💡 Takeaway: Trade during session overlaps when liquidity is at peak levels to get the tightest spreads and cleanest trends.

The 4 Primary Trading Styles

Match your personality and daily routine to the appropriate timeframe.

1. Scalping 1m - 5m charts

Seconds to minutes. Captures rapid 3–10 pip moves. Requires intense focus and zero distractions.

2. Day Trading 15m - 1H charts

Entered and exited in the same day. Eliminates overnight rollover swap fees and weekend gap risk.

3. Swing Trading 4H - Daily charts

Days to weeks. Captures major multi-hundred pip trends. The most flexible style for people working standard jobs.

4. Position Trading Daily - Weekly

Months to years. Relies strictly on central bank interest rate differentials and macro business cycles.

💡 Takeaway: If you work full-time, start with Swing Trading. Avoid scalping until you have mastered emotional regulation.

Fundamental vs. Technical Analysis

Combining the "Why" (economics) with the "Where" (charts).

The Fundamental Lens

Examines central bank rates, CPI (inflation), and GDP growth. Asks: "Is this country's economy strengthening or weakening relative to its peer?"

Tells you WHAT currency to buy or sell.
The Technical Lens

Examines price structure, support/resistance lines, and candlestick momentum. Asks: "Where are buyers defending this price level?"

Tells you WHEN and WHERE to enter and exit.
💡 Takeaway: Pro traders use Fundamentals to pick a direction, and Technicals to execute with minimal risk.

Risk Management: The Golden Rules

The mathematical foundation that separates long-term winners from blown accounts.

The 1% Rule

Never risk more than 1% to 2% of your account on one trade. If you have $2,000, max loss is $20.00.

1:2 Minimum R:R

Target at least $2 in reward for every $1 risked. You can lose 60% of trades and still make money!

Mandatory Stop Loss

A pre-placed order that cuts your loss automatically if the market invalidates your idea.

The 1:2 R:R Profitability Math:
• 4 Wins (4 × $200) = +$800
• 6 Losses (6 × $100) = -$600
• Net Profit = +$200 despite only a 40% win rate!
Level 2 • Module 5 of 10

Order Types & Execution Control

How to enter and protect trades without sitting at a screen all day.

Market Order

Fills instantly at current market price. Use when price action confirms and speed is crucial.

Limit Order (Buy/Sell)

Bargain hunter. Buys below current price or sells above current price on support/resistance pullbacks.

Stop Order (Buy/Sell)

Breakout hunter. Buys above current price or sells below once momentum shatters a key boundary.

Trailing Stop

A dynamic stop loss that automatically steps up with your profit, locking in gains while letting winners run.

💡 Takeaway: Set Limit orders at verified support zones so you don't chase fast-moving green candles.

4 Core Indicators Explained (No Math)

Tools that filter market noise into actionable statistical signals.

RSI (Relative Strength)

Oscillates 0 to 100. >70 is Overbought (likely due for pullback); <30 is Oversold (due for bounce).

50 & 200 EMAs

Moving average trend filters. 50 crossing above 200 = Golden Cross (strong uptrend signal).

MACD

A momentum meter that flags trend accelerations and early slowdowns before they become obvious.

ATR (True Range)

Measures average pip volatility. Tells you exactly how many pips wide your stop loss should be.

💡 Takeaway: Indicators are confirmation tools, not standalone signals. Price action and support/resistance always come first.

Top-Down Multi-Timeframe Analysis

Aligning multiple chart windows before risking capital.

1. Daily: Bird's Eye

Identifies the master trend direction and key institutional support/resistance levels.

2. 1-Hour: Strategy

Displays patterns (Double Bottom, flags, head & shoulders) forming inside that macro trend.

3. 15-Min: Sniper Trigger

Locates tight candlestick confirmations for the smallest possible stop loss and maximum R:R.

💡 Takeaway: Never fight the higher timeframe trend. If the Daily chart is trending up, only look for buy setups on the 15-minute chart.
Level 3 • Module 8 of 10

Economic Calendars & Red Folders

Surviving the volatility waves of scheduled central bank releases.

The Big 3 Releases

CPI (Inflation), NFP (Jobs), and FOMC (Interest rates) cause sudden 50–150 pip spikes.

The Surprise Factor

Markets react to the gap between Forecast and Actual numbers. A surprise miss sparks aggressive repricing.

Spread Widening

Liquidity dries up for seconds upon release. Spreads expand drastically, causing slippage. Do not gamble the second numbers drop.

💡 Takeaway: Check the live news tab before trading each morning to ensure you don't enter right before a red folder release.
Level 3 • Module 9 of 10

Trading Psychology & Mental Traps

Conquering the emotional impulses that cause 90% of account wipeouts.

Revenge Trading

Taking a loss and immediately firing another trade with double size to win it back. Step away from your screens instead!

FOMO Chasing

Watching an asset rocket upward and buying at the very peak out of fear of missing gains. Wait for the retest.

The Trading Journal

Record screenshot, setup, emotional state, and R:R for every single trade. Your journal is your true edge.

💡 Takeaway: If you take 2 consecutive losses in a session, close your charts for the day. Preserve mental capital.
